# Research Write-ups

Governs the shape of the document. It does not govern how to research.

## Rules

- List every URL referenced near the beginning of the file, before the
  findings.
- Do not begin planning an implementation. Research and implementation
  are separate requests.
- Do not put a summary at the end.
- When there were specific questions, answer those questions. Do not
  substitute a general overview of the topic.

## Shape

```markdown
# <Topic>

## Sources

- https://…
- https://…

## <Question or finding>

…
```
